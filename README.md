# Erly Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/erly-flow-rebuild-v2/**

Five ideas to turn more Erly users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Erly's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Make the paywall about their 6:30 AM | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Let them turn off one alarm first | +5–15% CR to purchase |
| 03 | Put 4.8 from 16K ratings where they decide | +5–10% CR to purchase |
| 04 | Get to the alarm sooner | +5–10% CR to purchase |
| 05 | Send everyone who backs out to your 33% offer | +5–10% ARPU |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Erly's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Erly. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
